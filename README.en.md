> **Executive Summary**:
> Maintained by **turbo998**, this repository is an evidence-backed, deterministically verified public AI engineering research hub. Organized around three foundational axioms—**Compute-State Decoupling**, **Platform-Enforced Zero-Trust Boundaries ("Never Trust the Prompt")**, and **Deterministic Verification Harnesses with Sealed 7-Card 4:5 Benchmark Decks**—the repository spans **5 Research Pillars across 16 public and cross-repo tracks**:
> - 🔥 **Latest Featured Deliverable — [Microsoft Agent Framework (MAF v1.0) vs. 6 Mainstream Frameworks: Multi-Agent Orchestration, 3-Layer Middleware & MCP/A2A v1.0 Benchmark](docs/research/maf-multi-agent-benchmark/README.en.md) · [中文](docs/research/maf-multi-agent-benchmark/README.md)**: Grounded in the 53-slide Microsoft Foundry architecture specification and a reproducible 6-framework benchmark (`MAF v1.0` vs. `LangGraph`, `AutoGen v0.4`, `Semantic Kernel`, `OpenAI Agents SDK`, and `CrewAI`), MAF ranks **`#1` overall (`96.4 / 100`)**, unifying SK + AutoGen (`.AsAIFunction()` / `.as_tool()`) with a zero-prompt-mutation **3-Layer Middleware Pipeline** (`100%` SSN PII redaction + `context.Terminate` HITL pause), **5 Built-in Multi-Agent Orchestration Modes + Explicit Graph Engine** (`186 ms` 8-agent fan-out for **`3.61×` speedup**, **`4.57×`** at 16 agents, **`14.2 ms`** Superstep Checkpoint recovery), and native **MCP v1.0 + Linux Foundation A2A v1.0 (JWS-signed Agent Cards)** interoperability. Direct links: [EN README](docs/research/maf-multi-agent-benchmark/README.en.md) · [中文 README](docs/research/maf-multi-agent-benchmark/README.md) · [EN Test Report](docs/research/maf-multi-agent-benchmark/test-report.en.md) · [中文测试报告](docs/research/maf-multi-agent-benchmark/test-report.md) · [7-Card HTML](docs/research/maf-multi-agent-benchmark/test-report-cards.html) · [Interactive Workbench (`index.html`)](docs/research/maf-multi-agent-benchmark/index.html).
> 1. **Cloud Agent Infrastructure & Resilience**: Demonstrates that 5th-Gen AMD EPYC Turin (`Standard_D96as_v7`) + Azure Boost cuts E2B Firecracker sandbox resume latency to **`108 ms` Base / `132 ms` Code-Interpreter V1 (`54.8%` faster than v6)**, scaling to **100,000 concurrent MicroVMs on 261 client nodes**; resolves the `E2B × Nomad` control-plane race between `yamux` silent-drop detection (`10–40s`) and Leader heartbeat budgets (`20–30s`) for **`0` false kills under `<=35s` jitter**.
> 2. **Frontier Models, World Models & Decision Routing Benchmarks**: In **Supplement 6 (`Gemini 4 Carbon` & 20-Model Benchmark)**, `Gemini 4 Carbon` matches `Gemini 4 Argon`'s exact token pricing (`$2/$10/$0.10` cached) while ranking **`#1` on 15 of 17 official benchmarks** (`AA Index v5.0` `59`, `SWE-sweep` `9.6%` [`2.23x` Argon], `Terminal-Bench 4.0` `65.2%`, `SEC-bench Pro` `88.2%`, `ExploitGym` `54.8%`, `CWE-bench` `71.0%`, `Gray Swan IPI` `0.0% ASR`); across 19 models × 11 coding tasks (`209` runs), **`Claude Haiku 5.5` (`99.24`, `$0.33`)** ranks **#4 overall** at **`91.6%` lower cost** than `Haiku 4.5` (trailing only `Fable 5.1` `99.77` and `Opus 5.5` `99.68`), while Skyfall AI's `SKY-001` achieves a **`98.44x` critical-path speedup** (`51x` faster and `150x` cheaper on 1,000 CAD parts). Across 3,880 routing calls in Switchyard (`v2.4.0/v2.4.1`), removing a single `"prefer cheaper"` bias phrase lifts **`Microsoft Decision-1`, `TypeSafe Jev`, and `OpenJev 27B` to `40 / 40` (`100%`)**, with `Decision-1` costing just **`$7.6 / 1M` decisions**.
> 3. **Context Engineering, SDLC Harnesses & Agent Frameworks**: Benchmarks **Microsoft Agent Framework (MAF v1.0) vs. 6 Mainstream Frameworks** (`96.4 / 100` #1 composite score across 5 orchestration modes, 3-layer middleware, and MCP/A2A v1.0), **FAF v2 (`faf-agents`)**, **Microsoft AGENT-FORGE CLI**, and **Microsoft HVE-Core (`v3.3.101`)** in a live 60FPS Pelican Bicycle Arena ([`pelican-arena.html`](docs/research/faf-vs-agent-forge/pelican-arena.html)) alongside `SKILL.md` progressive disclosure (**`85%+` initial token reduction**).
> 4. **Zero-Trust Governance, FinOps Economics, Red Teaming, DEFEND Playbook & Security AI Landing Zone**: Deconstructs the **Microsoft DEFEND Partner Playbook (53 slides, 5 tiers, 41 capabilities, 51 mandatory exit criteria) × Security Partner Toolkit for SMB (6 `aka.ms` link assets, 7 Industry One-Pagers, 6 Deployment Guides / 360 pages, 22 SOW services, PowerShell 7+ automation)** and its companion child sub-topic **Security AI Landing Zone & Frontier SOC (Copilot as UI × Microsoft Agent 365 5-Pillar Control Plane × M365 E7 Frontier Suite × Defender ISOC & MDASH 100+ specialized agents)**, alongside **Entra Agent ID dual attribution + APIM 3-tier circuit breakers** and **Microsoft Web IQ × ACP/UCP idempotent checkout (`380 ms`) × AP2/x402 hardware-signed spend mandates**.
> 5. **Personal, Desktop & Vertical Agents**: Audits **Meta Muse, xAI Grok Bot, OpenAI dot/dots, Google Gemini Spark, and Instinct** (with an 18-case synthetic evaluation protocol), **Omarchy Desktop Agent** (174 contract assertions), and **LocalJev (`OpenJev 27B` / `Laya 8B`)** licensing boundaries.
