## 2. Why Do Both Projects Share the Name "Agent Forge"?

- `customer-success-microsoft/faf-agents` uses the title **Agent Forge (FAF)** and marketplace slug **`agent-forge-plugins`** (evolving from Field Agent Framework / Factory Agent Forge inside MCAPS Customer Success).
- `microsoft/agent-forge` originated as **SpecForge**—as proven by line 8 of `src/lib/merger.ts`:
  ```typescript
  const GENERATED_MARKER = '<!-- Generated by AGENT-FORGE -->';
  const LEGACY_MARKER = '<!-- Generated by SpecForge -->';
  ```
  It was subsequently renamed to **AGENT-FORGE** and published publicly as `@agent-forge-copilot/cli`.

**One-Sentence Rule of Thumb**:
> - **FAF (`customer-success-microsoft/faf-agents`)** gives you **a pre-trained team of Microsoft Azure GenAI & Fabric solution consultants**;
> - **AGENT-FORGE (`microsoft/agent-forge`)** gives you **an automated factory that inspects your codebase and compiles tailored `.github/` Copilot agents and rules for it**.
