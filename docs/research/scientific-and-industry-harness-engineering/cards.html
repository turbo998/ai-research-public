7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS. NOT FOR & PRODUCTION READINESS CARD 7 OF 7

Industry Harnesses Must Pair Cognitive Calibrated Deference with Separated Validation & Structural Oracles

Actionable architectural verdict for AI Solution Architects building enterprise R&D, manufacturing, finance, and operations harnesses.

✅ Good Fit (High ROI Industry Scenarios)

1. Out-of-Distribution R&D with Imperfect Models Molecular/materials design, battery electrolytes, and novel semiconductor nodes where ML predictors have systematic offsets (~2.7 V) but useful local rank order.
2. Tri-Tier Grey-Box Enterprise Optimization Problems combining numerical scores (Tier A), multi-step process routes (Tier B), and natural-language physical/regulatory regimes (Tier C, e.g., pH 14 KOH, fab contamination).
3. Multi-Hour to Multi-Week Root-Cause Triage Fab yield crashes, grid/supply-chain disruptions, and clinical trial anomalies needing parallel hypothesis generation (Tiers 1–3) and minimum discriminating experiments.
4. Auditable Human–AI Shared Investigations Workflows requiring a persistent online belief graph (Accepted/Refuted/Open), mid-stream expert belief editing, and zero self-grading validation.

⛔ Not For (Anti-Patterns & Hard Boundaries)

1. Sub-100ms Real-Time Transactional Routing CLIO spawns recursive clean-context channels and runs GraphRAG + DRIFT clustering; use lightweight distilled routers for sub-second inline traffic.
2. Unchecked Structural Synthesis Without SMARTS/AST Gates As shown by RetroChimera's quinoline core swap on Compound 3, LLM deference alone can miss silent structural substitutions without a deterministic invariant checker.
3. Purely Opaque [0,1] Black-Box Number Crunching When zero domain physics, units, or thresholds are exposed, lightweight evolutionary mutation (ExLLM) reaches higher single-point black-box maxima (0.586 vs 0.544).
4. Uncalibrated Validation Prompts Vague requirements (“give a good answer”) cause premature false completion; impossible precision demands trigger 3-strike circuit breaker stalls.
Production Readiness & Reference Stack: Combine GitHub Copilot Base Harness + CLIO Recursive Belief Graph + Calibrated Deference Anchor Checks + Deterministic Structural Invariants (RDKit/AST) + Separated Validator (gpt-5.4 with 3-strike escalation).
